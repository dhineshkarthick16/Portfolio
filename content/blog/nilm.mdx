## Project Story

Non-Intrusive Load Monitoring (NILM) shifts the paradigm of smart metering from sub-metering every appliance to decomposing high-frequency aggregated current and voltage waveforms.

## The Engineering Behind It

We captured high-speed current transformer (CT) sensor data to build transient turn-on signatures for inductive and resistive loads.
