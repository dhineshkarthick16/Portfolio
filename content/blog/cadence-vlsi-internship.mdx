## The Origin & Objective

During my tenure at the Centre for Advanced Chip Design – Centre of Excellence, I immersed myself in the rigorous workflow of custom IC design. Moving beyond textbook digital logic into physical transistor sizing and layout parasitic extraction fundamentally changed how I view silicon engineering.

## What It Actually Looked Like

The core focus was implementing and benchmarking an ultra-low-power flip-flop topology in standard CMOS:

- **Schematic Capture**: Sizing PMOS and NMOS trees for minimal leakage during idle clock phases while preserving setup and hold time margins.
- **Custom Physical Layout**: Floorplanning to minimize parasitic interconnect capacitance and resistance.
- **Verification Loop**: Iterative Design Rule Checks (DRC) and Layout Versus Schematic (LVS) verification using Cadence Assura / Pegasus.

## Major Takeaways

1. **Parasitics Are Everything**: At advanced nodes, layout topology dictates power and delay as much as the schematic gate architecture.
2. **Verification Discipline**: Passing LVS is never just about connectivity — substrate taps, well proximity effects, and antenna rules demand attention early in the layout phase.
