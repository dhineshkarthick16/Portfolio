## Why We Built This

Highway commuting on two-wheelers in rural corridors is plagued by dead zones. Conventional emergency notification solutions rely on active cellular data to ping servers before an alert can even be parsed. We asked: *Can we detect a severe collision and trigger dispatch purely on-edge without touching the cloud?*

## The Prototyping Journey

We wired an MPU6050 6-axis IMU to an ESP32 dev kit. The primary challenge was distinguishing intentional emergency maneuvers (aggressive swerving, hard braking) from true impact and rollover events.

### The Protocol Hiccup

During bench testing, our companion mobile app was waiting on a multi-field CSV telemetry string, but the micro-controller emitted a bare `CRASH` flag on interrupt. This led to silent drops during early tests until we standardized a lightweight binary serialization format over BLE GATT characteristics.

## Next Steps

We are currently calibrating the complementary filter algorithms and exploring micro-LoRa beacons for true off-grid long-range distress signalling.
