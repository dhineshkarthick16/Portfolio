## Overview

A non-intrusive load monitoring (NILM) system built around an ESP32 and a
current-transformer sensing front-end, designed to detect and classify
appliance activity from a single monitoring point.

## Hardware

- ESP32
- SCT013-100A/1V (current transformer)
- LM358 (op-amp signal conditioning)
- 4N35 (optocoupler)
