## Overview

A Non-Intrusive Load Monitoring (NILM) platform engineered around an ESP32 dual-core microcontroller and an analog signal conditioning front-end. The system samples aggregate AC mains current non-invasively, synchronizes with voltage zero-crossings, and extracts true RMS metrics and power factor ($\cos \theta$) to decompose individual appliance energy consumption from a single measurement point.

## Hardware Front-End & Conditioning

- **Current Sensing:** SCT-013 non-invasive current transformer clamp (100A/50mA) with burden resistor converting induced secondary current to AC voltage.
- **Active DC Bias (+1.65V):** LM358 rail-to-rail operational amplifier configured as a unity-gain buffer to establish a low-impedance virtual ground, centering AC waveforms within the ESP32's 0V–3.3V ADC input window.
- **Galvanic Zero-Crossing Detection:** 4N35 optocoupler isolated from high-voltage line, generating digital interrupt pulses on mains voltage zero crossings for precise phase-angle alignment.
- **Compute:** ESP32 Xtensa Dual-Core 32-bit MCU (Core 0: DMA/FreeRTOS continuous ADC sampling; Core 1: True RMS and power factor extraction).

## Project Roadmap

- **✅ Phase 1 — Signal Conditioning & Phase Alignment (Current):**
  - LM358 +1.65V rail buffer design and noise decoupling
  - SCT-013 current calibration across dynamic loads
  - 4N35 optocoupler zero-crossing interrupt timing
  - FreeRTOS dual-core task segregation for high-frequency sampling without jitter
  - True RMS current ($I_{RMS}$), apparent power ($S$), and power factor ($\text{PF} = \cos \theta$) extraction
- **🚧 Phase 2 — Edge ML Load Disaggregation:**
  - Micro-transient turn-on signature library (inrush peaks, $dI/dt$, harmonic distortion)
  - Quantized TinyML edge classifier (1D-CNN or k-NN) for real-time appliance identification
  - Real-time energy telemetry streaming
